The road to Vice Got a Price! in six phases. Do them in order: story first, then strangers, then activities, then crime and money, then collectibles and cleanup. Each phase shows its trophies and how many you have earned in it.

No trophy is confirmed missable by every source. One is flagged as possibly missable, two more need your partner beside you. The honest position, with the disagreement stated, is below — plus the save habit that protects you against all three.

Save habit: several guides report there is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. That is the whole insurance policy.

Why only one possible missable?

GTABase and PowerPyx (via EGamers, 18 Nov 2026) flag Positive Mental Attitude as possibly missable: one meditation spot is reported to be available only in Chapter 1. PlanetGTA6 (18 Nov 2026) says it cannot be told yet — the game is a day old and no reliable missable list exists. This guide flags it as possible, says who says what, and tells you to meditate at every spot as you find it. The two partner trophies are not missable in the strict sense, but they need your partner available, so they are flagged as a caution and put in phase 2 of the roadmap. Everything else — chapters, missions, counts, collectibles — can be finished after the story.

Method — what this guide will and will not claim

  • Every guide is written in this guide's own words from the official description and the PSN target. No text is copied from any other guide site, wiki, video or forum post. Citing a source is allowed; copying it is not.
  • Where the official text does not pin down a place or a step (the cocktail bar, the showroom, the five cryptic trophies), the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location.
  • Five trophies have no confirmed trigger at launch (Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, plus the cocktail and showroom spots). They are marked “trigger unconfirmed at launch” on their own cards.
  • No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere in this app. All visuals are original CSS.
  • Everything runs offline from these files. The app makes no network requests at all — no fonts, no images, no analytics.
