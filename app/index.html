Roadmap to the Platinum — ~200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (early community estimate)

Six phases, in order. Finish the story first, then strangers, then activities and crime, then collectibles and the world, then money — the platinum pops when the last trophy does. No difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no online trophies. Cheats disable trophies (PowerPyx; Rockstar confirmed the same rule for GTA V), so keep cheats off until the platinum is yours.

Missable alerts — read this first

No trophy is confirmed missable by all sources. PlanetGTA6 (18 Nov 2026) says it cannot be told yet, and the game has no mission replay (OutputLag). One trophy is reported as possibly missable and two more need your partner beside you. Keep a few manual saves before big missions until the picture firms up.
